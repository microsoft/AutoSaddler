# Fixed-Current Preparation (Core)

Begin with history-analysis. Prepare the supplied current candidate in its isolated
workspace before this iteration's training evaluation. Do not choose a new base.
Use measured development vectors, archive membership, lessons, source references,
and recorded diffs to identify an evidence-backed local preparation.

Choose one of three actions: keep the fork unchanged; repair inherited quality loss
or cost growth without a compensating gain; or transfer a localized mechanism from
an eligible archive donor. Preserve existing improvements and explain the mechanism
and measured evidence. Do not copy an entire donor file/worktree, add unrelated
features, or invent a new diagnosis without this iteration's execution evidence.

Use the existing `.autosaddler/history/` candidate records, lineage, and immutable
diffs to compare eligible donor mechanisms with the current source. Stage only the
necessary edits in the current workspace. Donors do not become lineage parents.
Never reselect the parent, checkout/reset a donor, commit, or cherry-pick.

Follow the declared patch phase, scope, and verification skills. Do not use a
preparation label to evade steering restrictions. Preserve required work, task
validation, and benchmark isolation. Development/test case payloads and traces are
not preparation evidence. Use available development aggregates only.

Validate the resulting edit. Return the fixed parent, actual changed paths,
eligible donor IDs actually used, and an evidence-backed rationale. Keeping the
fork unchanged is a successful preparation with empty changed paths and donors.
The engine records edits, verifies and finalizes changed content, and evaluates
that exact prepared content as the before arm. Preparation does not promote a
candidate, update the archive, or replace current by itself.
